# Fishing Cat Data

Turns Roblox clan screenshots or a video into an Excel sheet.

## Quick start

- Google Gemini is picked by default.
- Paste your key. Tap "How to get a free key" to make one.
- Add screenshots or a video.
- Tap Make my sheet and save the file.

## Free key limit

The free Google Gemini key has a daily limit. If it runs out, wait until the next day, or use another company's key.

## Privacy

- Your key stays in this browser only. It is kept only if you tick Remember my key.
- Your pictures go only to the company you pick.
