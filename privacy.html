Privacy

Fishing Cat Data runs in your browser. We have no server and no database. We never see your pictures, files or keys.

AI company. Your pictures or bill photos go only to the AI company you pick (Google Gemini or Anthropic), using your own key.

Google sign-in. If you press Connect Google, the page asks for one permission: to make and change only the files it creates in your Google Drive (drive.file). The page can make a Google Sheet there with your results and update it later. It never opens, reads or changes your other files, email or contacts. You can delete that sheet in Drive any time. Your sign-in stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.

Discord. If you paste a Discord link, the page posts only what you choose to that channel.

Saved on your device. Anything saved stays in your browser. See or delete it in Settings, Privacy.

No tracking, no ads, no selling data.

AI company rules. Once your pictures reach Google Gemini or Anthropic, their own privacy rules apply.
