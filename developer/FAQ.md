# FAQ

## It says something went wrong on this page
Tap **Copy error** and send it to the author. Do not include your key.

## The sheet has wrong names or numbers
The AI can misread. Look for "?" and odd spellings, and fix cells in the table before you tap **Download sheet**.

## Some members are missing
The AI's reply may have been cut short. Try fewer pictures.

## How many pictures can I use?
Clan sheet: up to 30 files at a time, but accuracy may drop past about 20. Bills: 12 at most. Tap **Clear** to start again.

## Can I use a PDF bill?
Not yet. Bills are photos only.

## My gas bill is in m3 or CCF and was not converted
Without the conversion factors printed on the bill, it is flagged, not converted. The AI never does conversions.

## A bills run lost everything
If the AI's reply is cut off, the whole bills run is lost. Try fewer bills.

## Which AI should I use?
Gemini is the default and has a free key. Anthropic works. OpenAI is not tested.

## Does it cost money?
Every run uses credit on your own account with the AI company you pick. Short videos and fewer pictures cost less.

## Is my key saved?
Only if you tick **Remember my key**. Untick it to delete the saved key.

## Which Roblox games work?
Built for Gym League clans, and it also works for other Roblox games.

## Is it free to copy or reuse?
No. See the licence in the repo. Use the official site only.
