# FAQ

> [!TIP]
> Tap a question to open it.

<details>
<summary><b>It says something went wrong on this page</b></summary>

Tap **Copy error** and send it to the author. Do not include your key.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The sheet has wrong names or numbers</b></summary>

The AI can misread. Look for "?" and odd spellings, and fix cells in the table before you tap **Download sheet**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Some members are missing</b></summary>

The AI's reply may have been cut short. Try fewer pictures.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How many pictures can I use?</b></summary>

Clan sheet: up to 30 files at a time, but accuracy may drop past about 20. Bills: 12 at most. Tap **Clear** to start again.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use a PDF bill?</b></summary>

Not yet. Bills are photos only.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My gas bill is in m3 or CCF and was not converted</b></summary>

Without the conversion factors printed on the bill, it is flagged, not converted. The AI never does conversions.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>A bills run lost everything</b></summary>

If the AI's reply is cut off, the whole bills run is lost. Try fewer bills.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which AI should I use?</b></summary>

Gemini is the default and has a free key. Anthropic works. OpenAI is not tested.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it cost money?</b></summary>

Every run uses credit on your own account with the AI company you pick. Short videos and fewer pictures cost less.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is my key saved?</b></summary>

Only if you tick **Remember my key**. Untick it to delete the saved key.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which Roblox games work?</b></summary>

Built for Gym League clans, and it also works for other Roblox games.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free to copy or reuse?</b></summary>

No. See the licence in the repo. Use the official site only.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get a Discord webhook link?</b></summary>

In Discord: Server settings, Integrations, Create webhook, Copy webhook URL. [Discord's guide](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/228383668-Intro-to-Webhooks) has the details. If you can't see it, ask a Discord server admin to send you the link. Keep it private: anyone with it can post in your channel.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Posting to Discord fails</b></summary>

Check you copied the whole link. Turn off any ad blocker for this site, or use a Private window.

</details>
