Turn your Roblox clan list into a spreadsheet

Take screenshots of your clan's member list. This page reads them and gives you a spreadsheet file. Nobody has to type anything.

Bills works for power, gas, water, internet and phone. Photos or PDFs.
How to use it
  1. Get a free key from Google. It works like a password that lets this page read your pictures. It takes about 1 minute. Get my free key, then paste it in the box on the next screen.
  2. Add screenshots of your clan's member list, or a screen recording of you scrolling it slowly (about 1 second per 5 members).
  3. Optional: add last week's sheet. Then you see who gained, and it keeps track of everyone over time.
  4. Tap Make my sheet. After about 20 seconds an Excel file downloads.
What you get

A sample with made-up players. Yours will have your clan's names.

  • Clan Members: everyone's rank, role and points, with the change since last time.
  • Leaderboard: who gained the most, with gold, silver and bronze for the top 3.
  • Points history: one column per week, with a clan total.
  • About and How it's worked out: what every tab and colour means.
  • Bills: add photos or PDFs of power, gas, water, internet or phone bills and get one row per charge, plus a summary.
Is it safe?
  • Your key stays on your device. It is only saved if you tick Remember my key.
  • Only the company you pick (Google or Anthropic) sees your pictures. Nothing is saved on this site.

More on staying safe

Good to know
  • The free Google key has a daily limit. If it runs out, wait until tomorrow.
  • The AI reads the names and points. Check any ? or odd spelling by hand.
  • OpenAI is not tested yet and may not work.
  • Stuck? Tap Copy bug report in the box that appears while it works, and send it to the person who shared this page.

Keeping your AI key safe

    

  • Think of it like a credit card number: if someone uses your key, you get charged.
    • 

  • Never put it in a chat, email, post, screenshot or file you send. Anyone with it can use your credit.
    • 

  • Make a key just for this page and give it a clear name. Then you can delete it without breaking anything else.
    • 

  • This page only saves your key if you tick Remember my key. If you don't, refreshing or closing the tab clears it.
    • 

  • Ticked it? Untick the box to delete the saved key, and never do it on a shared computer.
    • 

  • If your browser or password manager offers to save it, choose "Never" unless you really want that.
    • 

  • Use your own device. Avoid shared or public computers, and close the tab when you're done.
    • 

  • Only install browser add-ons you trust. Add-ons can read what's on a page.
    • 

  • Think a key got out? Don't wait. Delete it (Anthropic): log in to your Anthropic account, open the API keys page from your profile, tap the three dots by the key and choose Delete API Key. Then make a new one and check your usage.

  • Another AI company? Use the keys page in your account with them.
    • 

  • Swap your key for a new one now and then. Anthropic's guide suggests about every 90 days.
    • 

  • If a key ends up in a public GitHub repo, GitHub tells Anthropic and the key is switched off automatically. Don't count on that.
    • 

  • Read Anthropic's own guide: API key best practices. It has the current steps.
