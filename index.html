Developer view
How to use it
  1. Paste your Anthropic AI key into the first box. Make one at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Pick your profile.
  3. Drop in screenshots of the clan member list, or a screen recording of you scrolling it.
  4. Tap Make my sheet and wait about 20 seconds. A CSV file downloads.
What you get
A CSV file named like "Loki - Gym League.csv" with four columns: Rank, Name, Role, Points. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Points are written as the game showed them, like 52.51K. Anyone who shows up in more than one picture is listed once.
Screenshots or video?
  • Screenshots: the whole member list, with the rows overlapping a little so nobody is missed.
  • Video (MP4, MOV or WEBM): the page picks about one picture every 2 seconds, up to 12. Scroll slowly. A very long video skips more of the list, so keep it short or use screenshots.
  • Rows cut off at the top or bottom edge are skipped.
If something goes wrong
  • The AI said no: the key is wrong, expired, or out of credit. Paste it again.
  • No member rows were found: the pictures don't show the member list. Try clearer ones.
  • This video could not be opened: try an MP4 recording.
  • A "?" in the sheet means a name or number was unreadable. Fix it by hand.
  • Still stuck: open Updates, tap Developer view at the bottom of the first tab, and send me a screenshot of the Console.
