Screenshots in, spreadsheet out

Take pictures of a Roblox clan list or a household bill. An AI reads them and gives you an Excel file.

  1. Get a free key from Google (about a minute). It lets the page read your pictures. Get my free key, then paste it on the next screen.
  2. Add your pictures, or a slow screen recording.
  3. Tap Make my sheet. The file downloads in about 20 seconds.

Want to see who gained points? Add last week's sheet too.

See an example

Made-up samples. Tap a tab to see it. Yours will have your own names and numbers.

  • Clan Members: everyone's rank, role and points, with the change since last time.
  • Top 10: the best 10 this month and each week, with gold, silver and bronze for the top 3.
  • Points history: one row per player, every read side by side, with a clan total. With only two days saved, it says the same as “What changed”; that’s normal.
  • Help: what every word, tab and colour means, with the maths step by step.
  • Bills: add photos or PDFs of power, gas, water, internet or phone bills and get one row per charge, plus a summary.

Stay safe

    

  • Only use the real link: fishing-cat-data.pages.dev. Copies on other addresses can steal your key.
    • 

  • The AI can misread. Check the names and points in the sheet before you act on it or share it.
    • 

  • Google sign-in happens on Google's own page. This page never sees your password and never keeps your sign-in.
    • 



Your AI key

    

  • Treat it like a card number: whoever has it can spend your credit.
    • 

  • Never put it in a chat, email, post or screenshot.
    • 

  • Make a key just for this page, so you can delete it on its own.
    • 

  • Don't tick Remember my key on a shared computer. Say "Never" if your browser offers to save it.
    • 

  • Only install browser add-ons you trust.
    • 

  • Swap it for a new key about every 90 days. Anthropic's guide
    • 



Real page or fake?

    

  • Copies of this page can steal keys. Check the address bar says fishing-cat-data.pages.dev and bookmark it.
    • 

  • On another address the page shows a red warning and locks the key box, but a copy can remove that.
    • 

  • Be careful with links from strangers.
    • 



Costs

    

  • Each run uses credit on your own AI account. Check your usage now and then, and set a spending limit if offered.
    • 

  • Fewer pictures and shorter videos cost less.
    • 



Names and the sheet

    

  • The usernames belong to real people. Share the sheet only with people who should see it.
    • 

  • Look for "?" and odd spellings, and take the page's warnings seriously before deciding anything about members.
    • 



Key leaked?

    

  • Delete it now in your AI account's API keys page (Anthropic: profile, API keys, three dots, Delete).
    • 

  • Make a new key, check your usage, and tell the author what happened.

Privacy

    

  • Your AI key stays in this browser. Only kept if you tick Remember my key. Only sent to the AI company you pick.
    • 

  • The page can only talk to Anthropic, Google and Discord (locked by the browser). It only sends to the AI company you pick.
    • 

  • Pictures go only to your AI company, including stills pulled from a video, straight from your browser. This page has no server, so we never see them.
    • 

  • Discord is used only if you add a Discord link on the Discord tab and tap Post to Discord. It sends the clan name, points summary and sheet to your channel.
    • 

  • Saved on this device: theme, text size, and the last sheet you chose (names, points, past runs). Settings, Privacy shows it, deletes it, or stops saving the sheet.
    • 

  • The AI company's own rules apply to what you send. Free plans can differ, so check them.
    • 

  • The website host may keep normal visit records, like any website host.
    • 

  • Bills: the whole photo goes to the AI company, including your name, address and account number. The page asks the AI not to copy them, and the sheet has no place for them.
    • 

  • The names in your pictures belong to real people. Share the sheet only with people who should see it.
