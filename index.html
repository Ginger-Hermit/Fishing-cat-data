How to use it
  1. Paste your AI key. Make one at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Pick your profile.
  3. Drop in screenshots of the member list, or a screen recording of you scrolling it.
  4. Optional: add last time's sheet to compare.
  5. Tap Make my sheet. After about 20 seconds an Excel file downloads.
What you get
  • An Excel file like "Loki - Gym League.xlsx".
  • Points twice: as the game showed them (52.51K) and as a real number (52510).
  • With a last sheet: Previous Points, Change, % Change and a Trend arrow.
  • A second tab explains each column.
Screenshots or video?
  • Screenshots: show the whole list, overlapping a little so nobody is missed.
  • Video (MP4, MOV, WEBM): about one picture every 2 seconds, up to 12. Scroll slowly and keep it short.
  • Rows cut off at the top or bottom edge are skipped.
Not working?
  • The AI said no: the key is wrong, expired or out of credit.
  • No member rows found: the pictures don't show the member list.
  • Video could not be opened: try an MP4.
  • A "?" in the sheet: unreadable. Fix it by hand.
  • Still stuck: tap Developer view on the first tab and send a screenshot of the Console.
