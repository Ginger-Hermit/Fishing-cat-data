What it can and can't do
What it can do
  • Make a clan sheet for any Roblox game and show who gained points since last time.
  • Turn power, gas, water, internet and phone bills into one row per charge, plus a summary.
  • Post the result to your Discord channel, with a table picture.
  • It's free to use. You bring your own AI key (a free one works).
What it can't do
  • It doesn't connect to Roblox or log in anywhere. It only reads the pictures you give it.
  • The AI can misread a name or number, so give the sheet a quick look.
  • It has no accounts and keeps nothing online. Your history lives in the Excel file you keep.
  • It isn't financial advice, and it isn't an official Roblox, Google or Anthropic product.
How to use it
  1. Get a free key from Google. It works like a password that lets this page read your pictures. It takes about 1 minute. Get my free key, then paste it in the box on the next screen.
  2. Add screenshots of your clan's member list, or a screen recording of you scrolling it slowly (about 1 second per 5 members).
  3. Optional: add last week's sheet. Then you see who gained, and it keeps track of everyone over time.
  4. Tap Make my sheet. After about 20 seconds an Excel file downloads.
What you get

Made-up samples. Tap a tab to see it. Yours will have your own names and numbers.

  • Clan Members: everyone's rank, role and points, with the change since last time.
  • Top 10: the best 10 this month and each week, with gold, silver and bronze for the top 3.
  • Points history: one row per player, every read side by side, with a clan total.
  • Help: what every word, tab and colour means, with the maths step by step.
  • Bills: add photos or PDFs of power, gas, water, internet or phone bills and get one row per charge, plus a summary.
Is it safe?
  • Your key stays on your device. It is only saved if you tick Remember my key.
  • Only the company you pick (Google or Anthropic) sees your pictures. Nothing is saved on this site.

More on staying safe

Good to know
  • The free Google key has a daily limit. If it runs out, wait until tomorrow.
  • The AI reads the names and points. Check any ? or odd spelling by hand.
  • OpenAI is not tested yet and may not work.
  • Stuck? Tap Copy bug report in the box that appears while it works, and send it to the person who shared this page.

Privacy

    

  • Your AI key stays in this browser. It is only kept if you tick Remember my key, and it is never sent anywhere except the AI company you pick.

Discord is used only if you add a Discord link on the Discord tab and tap Post to Discord. That sends the clan name, points summary and sheet to your channel.

  • Your pictures (and the stills pulled from a video) go only to the AI company you pick, straight from your browser, to be read. This page has no server of its own, so we never see them.
    • 

  • What that company does with what you send is covered by its own policies. Free plans can have different rules, so check them.

  • Saved on your device only: theme, text size, and the last sheet you chose (names, points and past runs). Settings has a Privacy section that lists what is saved and can delete it, or stop saving the last sheet.

    • 

  • Bills: the whole photo goes to the AI company, including your name, address and account number. The page asks the AI not to copy those, and the sheet has no place for them.
    • 

  • The names in your pictures belong to real people. Share the sheet only with people who should see it.
