How to use it
  1. Paste your AI key. Make one at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Pick your profile.
  3. Drop in screenshots of the member list, or a screen recording of you scrolling it.
  4. Optional: add last time's sheet to compare.
  5. Tap Make my sheet. After about 20 seconds an Excel file downloads.
What you get
  • An Excel file like "Loki - Gym League.xlsx".
  • Points twice: as the game showed them (52.51K) and as a real number (52510).
  • With a last sheet: Previous Points, Change, % Change and a Trend arrow.
  • A second tab explains each column.
Screenshots or video?
  • Screenshots: show the whole list, overlapping a little so nobody is missed.
  • Video (MP4, MOV, WEBM): about one picture every 2 seconds, up to 12. Scroll slowly and keep it short.
  • Rows cut off at the top or bottom edge are skipped.
Not working?
  • A red message about your key, credit or too many requests: fix the key or credit, or wait a minute.
  • No member rows found: the pictures don't show the member list.
  • Video could not be opened: try an MP4.
  • A "?" in the sheet: unreadable. Fix it by hand.
  • Still stuck: tap Developer view on the first tab and send a screenshot of the Console.

Keeping your AI key safe
  • Never put it in a chat, email, post, screenshot or file you send. Anyone with it can use your credit.
  • Make a key just for this page and give it a clear name. Then you can delete it without breaking anything else.
  • This page never saves your key. Refreshing or closing the tab clears it.
  • If your browser or password manager offers to save it, choose "Never" unless you really want that.
  • Use your own device. Avoid shared or public computers, and close the tab when you're done.
  • Only install browser add-ons you trust. Add-ons can read what's on a page.
  • Think a key got out? Don't wait. Delete it in your Anthropic account, make a new one, and check your usage.
Real page or fake?
  • Anyone can copy a web page and change it to steal keys. Only paste your key on the real link: fishing-cat-data.pages.dev
  • Check the address bar before you paste. Bookmark the real link.
  • Be careful with links sent by strangers, even if the page looks the same.
  • This page will never ask for your password, your email or anything except the AI key.
